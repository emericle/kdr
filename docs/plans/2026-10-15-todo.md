## Phase P — Persist market data across upgrades (migrate, never drop)

Goal: `market_data`, `sentiment_data` and `model_weights` survive every schema
upgrade, and the store is kept for as long as possible. No code path may drop or
recreate a non-empty database.

## Risk and ordering

Phase P is the highest-risk, highest-value change: it decides how schema evolution
touches a live store, so land it (and its contract test) before any Perf/UX change
that adds a column or table. Perf is mechanical. UX is user-visible but low risk. The
Hygiene batch is independent and can land first.
